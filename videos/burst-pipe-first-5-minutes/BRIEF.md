## Intent

An English, premium, upbeat know-how explainer: "Pipe Burst? Your First 5 Minutes." The viewer should walk away knowing exactly what to do — in order — if a pipe bursts at home, and do one thing today (find and tag the main shut-off valve). Polished and high-end (not cheap/cartoony), lively and fast-paced. Published to YouTube, Facebook and X. Direct MP4 delivery, no review stops.

## Notes

Facts (all on-screen figures trace here):
- Triple-I (Insurance Information Institute), ISO/Verisk data 2019–2023: about 1 in 67 insured homes has a water damage or freezing claim each year; average claim severity $15,400.
- Guidance synthesized from Red Cross, Forbes Home and licensed plumbers: shut off the main valve first (usually where the main line enters: basement, crawl space, utility room, near the water meter; gate/wheel valve clockwise, ball/lever valve a quarter turn so the handle crosses the pipe); cut power to affected areas at the breaker only if you can reach it dry — never stand in water (Red Cross); open faucets hot & cold and flush toilets to drain lines; turn off the water heater; photograph/video damage before cleanup; move valuables; call a licensed plumber and the insurer; tape/clamps won't hold at full pressure.
- Deliberately NOT used: the "1/8-inch crack leaks 250 gallons a day" claim (sources disagree by >10×).
